# Beyond Rubber Ducking - TechLead Conf. 2026 - London

**What Happens When the Duck Talks Back**

## Overview

We used to explain our bugs to rubber ducks. Now the duck talks back... and codes. This talk examines both the genuine benefits and hidden human costs of AI-assisted development, providing Tech Leads and Engineering Managers with evidence-based frameworks to protect their teams' growth, motivation, and long-term capability.

## Target Audience

- Engineering Managers & Tech Leads
- CTOs & Directors of Engineering
- Senior Engineers navigating AI adoption
- Anyone responsible for team capability and developer growth

## Key Themes

### The Balance
- **Real Benefits**: Performance gains, legacy code explanation, 24/7 availability, objective code review
- **Hidden Costs**: Flow state destruction, learned helplessness, cognitive debt, motivation loss
- **The Paradox**: AI makes us more productive and less motivated (11% motivation drop, 20% boredom increase - HBR study)

### The Human Impact
- **Flow State**: How AI delegation kills the deep engagement that makes us love coding
- **Cognitive Debt**: Understanding gaps, decision fatigue, and learning loss that accumulate over time
- **Junior Vulnerability**: The validation paradox - you need expertise to validate AI, but can't build expertise if AI does the work

### The Framework
A 6-point Engagement Plan for Tech Leads & EMs:
1. **Regular Check-ins**: "What did you learn?" vs "What did you ship?"
2. **Code Review Evolution**: Celebrate understanding, not just acceptance
3. **Protect Flow**: Designated blocks for deep work, challenge-rich tasks for humans
4. **Mentorship Programs**: Pair juniors with seniors, not just with AI
5. **Team Ceremonies**: Share victories and learnings together
6. **Celebrate Creativity**: Value the craft, not just output
