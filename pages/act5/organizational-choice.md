# The Organizational Choice

::left::

<v-clicks>

**Path 1: Acceleration Only**
- ❌ Ship faster, understand less
- ❌ Growing knowledge debt
- ❌ Team dependency increases
- ❌ Capability erosion

</v-clicks>

::right::

<v-clicks>

**Path 2: Upskilling First**
- ✅ Learn with AI as assistant
- ✅ Maintain expertise
- ✅ Speed AND understanding
- ✅ Sustainable advantage

</v-clicks>
